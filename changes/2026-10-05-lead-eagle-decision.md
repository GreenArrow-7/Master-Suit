# Lead Eagle: build it inside YOUHAN ONE

**Decision (owner, 5 Oct).** Lead Eagle's features become a module inside YOUHAN
ONE, on the same records as Sales and Real Estate, sold from the platform
portal. Earlier the same day #123 — Lead Eagle as a plan module linking out to
the separate app — was closed to keep Lead Eagle standalone; this decision
supersedes that.

**Context.** Lead Eagle is a separate repository on the owner's machine (beside
this one): a real-estate sales CRM for the UAE on the same stack (Next.js 16,
Prisma 7, Postgres row-level security) with an Expo phone app. In September its
portal feeds, lead recycling and branded proposals were ported into YOUHAN ONE's
Real Estate module; its face attendance and live-call help were judged weaker
than YOUHAN ONE's and were not. On 4 Oct it was made standalone again.
