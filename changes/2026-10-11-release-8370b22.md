**What.** Production moved from b28d2b7 to 8370b222 on 11 Oct, at about
04:20 UTC. Migrations went from 98 to 101:
`20261011000000_lead_contact_sensitive`,
`20261011000100_triage_owner_deactivated` and

**What customers see on the day.** Nobody's view narrows. The two grant
migrations give every role that reads leads the sensitive-fields permission
and every role that views reports the export permission, so phone numbers stay
whole and report downloads keep working until an administrator switches a role
off. A lead's secondary phone, secondary email and address, withheld from
everyone until now (the check named a permission no role could hold), now show
to every role that reads leads.
