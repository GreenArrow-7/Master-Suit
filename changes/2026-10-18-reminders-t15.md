# Reminders ring fifteen minutes before

**18 Oct 2026** · change · Lead Eagle, follow-ups, notifications (audit M8-05; lane reminders-15)

**What.** PR #218. The reminder sweep looks 15 minutes ahead instead of 45 and runs every five minutes instead of every fifteen, so a follow-up, a call follow-up or a site visit rings its owner 10–15 minutes before it is due (T-15) instead of 30–45.

**Why.** Audit row M8-05 ("fires 15 minutes before the due time"); the owner chose T-15 (approved build, 18 Oct).

**Where.**

- `src/services/crm/reminders.ts` — `LOOKAHEAD_MINUTES = 15`; the three sweeps, the once-per-recipient/kind/record check and the push/email copies (`record-push`) are unchanged.
- `src/workers/maintenance.ts` — the `reminders` job is armed as `reminders-five-minutely` (`*/5 * * * *`); `RETIRED_SCHEDULES` removes the old `reminders-quarter-hourly` scheduler at each worker start, since `upsertJobScheduler` never drops an id it is not given and the old one would keep firing beside the new.

**Behaviour.** The bell, push and email reminder arrive about a quarter of an hour before the follow-up, call follow-up or visit, not three quarters. The look-ahead is three times the cadence, so a late or skipped run (up to two in a row) still catches the item; nothing is sent twice, because the sweep still skips anyone already told about that record in the last 24 hours. The worker's start-up line names `reminders-five-minutely`.

**Verified.** `tests/integration/follow-up-reminder.spec.ts` (3, each failing on the old code): a follow-up due in 20 minutes rings nothing, one due in 10 rings exactly once over two sweeps; the same for site visits; the scheduler arms `reminders-five-minutely` at `*/5 * * * *`, not `reminders-quarter-hourly`, and removes the retired id. With the specs of the touched areas (field-security's call reminder at 10 minutes, the digest, SLA, report-schedule and score-decay scheduler cases): 86/86 in 6 files; `tsc` clean.

**Decisions taken.** T-15 with a five-minute sweep (owner default YES), as the audit suggested: one constant, one schedule row. A reminder therefore lands between 15 and 10 minutes before, not on the minute — exact T-15 would need a per-item delayed job, not worth it for a bell. The schedule id was renamed to say what it does, and the old one is removed by name rather than left firing.

**Left open.** Nothing for this row. A per-workspace or per-person offset is not built (no one asked).
