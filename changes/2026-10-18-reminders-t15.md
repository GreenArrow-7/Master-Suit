# Reminders ring fifteen minutes before

**18 Oct 2026** · change · Lead Eagle, follow-ups, notifications (audit M8-05; lane reminders-15)

**What.** PR #218. The reminder sweep looks 15 minutes ahead instead of 45 and runs every five minutes instead of every fifteen, on a queue of its own instead of the maintenance queue, so a follow-up, a call follow-up or a site visit rings its owner 10–15 minutes before it is due (T-15) instead of 30–45.

**Why.** Audit row M8-05 ("fires 15 minutes before the due time"); the owner chose T-15 (approved build, 18 Oct).

**Where.**

- `src/services/crm/reminders.ts` — `LOOKAHEAD_MINUTES = 15`; the three sweeps, the once-per-recipient/kind/record check and the push/email copies (`record-push`) are unchanged.
- `src/workers/maintenance.ts` — the sweep leaves `MAINTENANCE_JOBS` for `REMINDER_JOBS` (job `sweep`), armed on the `reminders` queue as `reminders-five-minutely` (`*/5 * * * *`); `RETIRED_SCHEDULES` removes the old `reminders-quarter-hourly` scheduler from the maintenance queue at each worker start, since `upsertJobScheduler` never drops an id it is not given and the old one would keep firing beside the new.
- `src/lib/queue.ts`, `src/workers/jobs.ts` — the `reminders` queue (one attempt: the next sweep is the retry) and its consumer, one job at a time like every queue but `ai`.
- `infra/prometheus-alerts.yml` (QueueHasNoConsumer covers `reminders`), `docs/DEPLOY-AZURE.md` (the start-up line lists ten queues), `docs/product/RELEASE-RUNBOOK.md`.

**Behaviour.** The bell, push and email reminder arrive about a quarter of an hour before the follow-up, call follow-up or visit, not three quarters. The look-ahead is three times the cadence, so a sweep up to ten minutes late, or two skipped in a row, still catches the item. Past ten minutes late an item falls due before any sweep has seen it and never rings, so the sweep no longer queues behind the maintenance worker's one-at-a-time jobs (the hourly digest, the 03:00 retention sweep, the report emails), any of which could hold it that long; nothing is sent twice, because the sweep still skips anyone already told about that record in the last 24 hours. The worker's start-up line names `reminders-five-minutely`.

**Verified.** `tests/integration/follow-up-reminder.spec.ts` (3, each failing on the old code): a follow-up due in 20 minutes rings nothing, one due in 10 rings exactly once over two sweeps; the same for site visits; the sweep is the `reminders` queue's `sweep` job and no longer a maintenance job, armed on that queue as `reminders-five-minutely` at `*/5 * * * *`, and the retired `reminders-quarter-hourly` id is removed (failed on the first review head, where the sweep still queued behind the maintenance jobs). With the specs of the touched areas (field-security's call reminder at 10 minutes, the digest, SLA, report-schedule and score-decay scheduler cases, the metrics queue list, the account-deletion and triage workers): 124/124 in 11 files; `check:observability` ok (it failed until QueueHasNoConsumer named `reminders`); `tsc` clean.

**Decisions taken.** T-15 with a five-minute sweep (owner default YES), as the audit suggested: one constant, one schedule row. A reminder therefore lands between 15 and 10 minutes before, not on the minute — exact T-15 would need a per-item delayed job, not worth it for a bell. The schedule id was renamed to say what it does, and the old one is removed by name rather than left firing. A queue of its own rather than a BullMQ priority on the other maintenance schedules (review of #218): a priority only orders waiting jobs, and a digest or retention run already in progress would still hold the sweep. A stray `reminders` job left on the maintenance queue across the deploy is logged as unknown and dropped; the next five-minute sweep covers its window.

**Left open.** Nothing for this row. A per-workspace or per-person offset is not built (no one asked).
