# A manager sees their whole line; quotas, languages, availability and shares steer assignment; Lead Eagle writes its own automations

**18 Oct 2026** · feature · Lead Eagle, allocation, automation, follow-ups (audit M1-12, M7-02, M7-04, M7-06, M7-07, M7-11, M7-12, M8-01; lane assignment-followups)

**What.** PR #206.

- **Reporting line (M1-12).** TEAM scope's `managedUserIds` walks `User.managerId` down every level (ten at most), not only direct reports: a director sees the reps of the managers who report to them. "Reports to" is set under Admin → Users → the account → **Lead work**, which refuses a loop (409).
- **Lead work on the account (M7-04, M7-06, M7-07).** The same form sets the daily lead quota (blank = no limit), the languages the person sells in, and "Available for new leads". New action `POST /api/v1/workspaces/{slug}/identity/account-lead-settings` (`users:MANAGE_USERS`, only for an account strictly below the caller's rank, never their own; audited). The person switches their own availability on My role & access (`POST …/identity/self/lead-availability`). Eligibility already read all four; nothing wrote them.
- **Weighted turns (M7-02).** A rule's `weights` ({userId: 1–10}) give a person that many turns per round, spread out (A×2 over A, B, C turns A, B, C, A). The pointer is the new `DistributionRule.rotationSlot` (migration `20261018093700_distribution_rule_rotation_slot`, additive); an unweighted rule still turns on `lastAssignedUserId`. Allocation → Automatic assignment: picking people shows a "turns per round" box for each.
- **Language routing (M7-04).** A rule's `conditions.languages` takes leads tagged with one of them (case-insensitive) and hands them only to people whose languages include it; anyone else in the pool is passed over, an untagged lead falls through to the next rule.
- **"Marked unavailable" says so.** `MARKED_UNAVAILABLE` is no longer redacted to "unavailable" in the assignment sentence: it is the person's own switch, not an HR record. Leave and employment stay redacted.
- **Automation in every lead module (M7-11).** `/api/v1/automations` (POST, PATCH) and the Automation page admit `LEAD_MODULES`; `/lead-eagle/automation` re-exports it, with a menu entry under Administration. A trigger may be **some time after a lead arrives** (`event: 'time'`, `afterMinutes`), optionally "only if nobody has contacted it yet" (a `firstContactedAt is_null` condition): the maintenance job `automation-time` (every five minutes) enrolls each lead once.
- **Actions (M7-12).** `assign` (a named person, checked as a hand-over by the author; or none: the next in turn under the rules, for a lead nobody holds), `send_template` (an email or approved WhatsApp template through the lead's own send), `webhook` (https only; POST signed `X-Youhan-Signature: sha256=<HMAC of the body>` with a secret shown once on creation and kept sealed in the spec; refused for an address inside the network, redirects refused; the payload names the record, never its numbers or email). The engine runs every action as the automation's author, so a webhook carries only what that author may open: creating one needs `VIEW` on the record type (`leads`, `contacts`, `accounts`, `opportunities`; 403 otherwise, so an IT administrator with `automation:MANAGE_AUTOMATION` and no lead access cannot export leads), and each run checks the record against the author's scope (`assertLeadInScope` / `assertRecordInScope` at `VIEW`) and sends nothing when it is out of reach — including a webhook whose author has since lost access. `notify_manager` now notifies the owner's manager (`User.managerId`), not the owner. Creation answers `idempotent: false` (the secret is in the answer).
- **Follow-up kind (M8-01).** The Follow-ups list shows a Type column (Call, Meeting, Site visit, Other; on by default) and a Type filter row beside the due tabs; a filter keeps the others.

**Decisions taken.** Weighted assignment and language routing built small (owner default): weights on the rule; languages on `User.languages`, which existed, with an edit field on the user form; the lead's language is read from its tags (`ponytail:` note in `assignLead.ts`). Time triggers are "N minutes after the lead was created", leads only, no backfill past a day (`ponytail:` note in `engine.ts`). The webhook payload names the record and leaves contact details out. Creating a webhook needs `VIEW` on its record type, not an integrations grant: every run is held to the author's own record scope, so it sends nothing the author could not already read through the API (raised in review).
