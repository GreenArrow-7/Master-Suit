# A provider's calls on the lead's timeline, a call report per agent, missed-call notices and tracking numbers

**18 Oct 2026** · feature · Lead Eagle, calls, telephony (audit M9-02, M9-03, M9-05, M9-06, M9-07, M9-08, PROP-05; umbrella OWN-06)

**What.** PR #205, branch `feat/le-calls`.

- **Timeline (M9-03).** The lead's timeline already listed each `Call` row (direction, outcome, minutes, since #178); each call entry now opens its call page: _Play recording_ once the recording is in our storage and its consent stands (as the media route plays it), else _Open call_. The Calls list takes `?lead=<id>` (named at the top, kept on every tab and page), and the lead's timeline has an _All calls_ link for a role with `calls:VIEW`.
- **Recordings list (M9-07).** The Calls list has a _Recorded_ tab: the calls whose recording is in our own storage with consent given and not withdrawn, to play from the call page.
- **Connected without logging (PROP-05).** A provider call that ends (`CALL_COMPLETED`) after the vendor saw it answered is `CONNECTED` unless somebody recorded an outcome; where the vendor reports talk time, that decides (no talk, not connected). An answered callback that arrives after the completed one still records the answer and, with no talk time, makes the call connected; Knowlarity, which sends no answered callback, says it on the end-of-call event (`dispatch_status: answered`, `CallEvent.answered`). The vendor's talk time is kept in the new `Call.talkSecs` (migration `20261018104500_call_talk_secs`).
- **Call performance (M9-05).** Reports → Team & Users → _Call Performance_: per agent, calls (placed through the provider and logged by hand, as the board counts them), connected, connect rate, time on calls, average connected call, and the provider's talk time; From/To on the workspace's clock and CSV for `reports:EXPORT`. Every report now has the From/To form. The board's Calls tile links to it.
- **Missed calls (M9-08).** An inbound call that ends missed or busy rings the lead's owner (HIGH, bell and push, _Missed call from <lead>_), once. A lead with no owner yet (an unknown caller's, #177, until distribution names one) has the notice go to the person the call is recorded against (a contact's owner with that number, else the most senior user), as _Missed call from an unassigned lead_ linking the call, not the lead they may not see. When distribution then assigns the lead, its agent gets _Lead assigned_ and the HIGH _Missed call from <lead>_.
- **Tracking numbers (M9-06).** Admin → Integrations → each telephony vendor: _Tracking numbers_, `+97144000001 = Google Ads; +97144000002 = Marina launch`. A new caller to a tagged number becomes a lead from the Sources entry of that name (Settings → Sources), detail `<vendor>:<that number>`, its note _Enquiry via Telephony · Google Ads_.
- **Post-call log on a provider call (M9-02).** The lead list's one-click call log (#178) already wrote outcome and follow-up in one save. When a provider call placed from the live call screen ends, that dialog now opens by itself; given the call's id, the log writes the outcome onto that call (no second call logged), adds its note after the notes taken during the call, and books the follow-up on that call (its page lists it). Logging onto a call takes `calls:EDIT`, as editing the call does; without it the call answers 404.

**Decisions taken** (design choices, none of the owner's listed defaults applies to these rows)

- Connected is decided when the call ends: talk time where the vendor reports it, else "answered". An existing lead keeps its first source when it calls a tracking number; only a new caller's lead takes the tag.
- A tag names a Sources entry by name, in any case; a tag no entry carries stays on the note and the source stays Telephony.
- The report counts logged calls beside the provider's, as the board does; the average is over connected calls; talk time is only what a vendor reports. It reads under both the reports and the calls scope.
- A call log on a provider call scores nothing: the call is already on the timeline, and the score moves only for a logged activity.
- Review follow-up: the dialog's note goes after the call's own notes on a new line, not over them; a role without `calls:EDIT` gets 404 for the call (the route's not-yours answer), not 403; the live call screen already needs `calls:EDIT`, so its dialog is unaffected.
- Review follow-up: an inbound missed call on a lead nobody owns names no lead and links the call (the recipient's own record), for the senior user too, since the notice cannot tell whether its recipient sees the lead; the agent distribution picks later gets the named notice for the lead's latest missed inbound call, so the senior user and the agent can both hear of one call.
- Review follow-up: a late answered callback keeps the first answered time it sees and decides the outcome only where no talk time was reported; Knowlarity's "completed" alone is not taken as answered.

**Left open**

- **Owner:** a Twilio (or Exotel, Knowlarity, Plivo) account, connected on staging, and one real call round trip: status, duration, talk time, recording ingested and played (OWN-06, M9-07).
- **Owner decision (M9-09):** detecting calls made on the handset needs a native Android module holding the call-log permissions, which Google Play allows only for the default dialler (or an approved exception), at the risk of the listing; iOS cannot read the call history at all. The phone app is a web view today. Not built.
- Twilio's click-to-call reports the agent's leg, so its "answered" is the agent picking up; reading the client's leg (`<Dial action>`) is the upgrade if that misleads (`ponytail:` in the webhook).
- The live screen polls the call every 5 s for its end; a pushed status event when the stream engine has one.
- Tracking numbers are one 500-character setting (about a dozen lines); a table of their own if a workspace runs more.
