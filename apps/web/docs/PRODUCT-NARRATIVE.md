# YOUHAN ONE — problem, impact, solution, outcome

The product story in the order a buyer hears it. Every figure the product shows in
support of this story is a count of work it actually did (`src/lib/value/platformValue.ts`);
this document never states a number the platform has not measured.

## Problem

A sales organisation runs on a handful of people doing the same manual work every
day: leads arrive in spreadsheets and chat threads and are typed in by hand; calls
happen on personal phones and are remembered, not recorded; follow-ups live in
heads and notebooks; attendance is a register; the owner learns what happened to
last week's leads by asking. Each of those is a place where a lead is lost, a
follow-up is missed, or the owner cannot see who did what.

## Business impact

The cost is time and lost deals, not software: hours a seller spends typing and
re-typing instead of talking to buyers; leads that were assigned but never
contacted; calls nobody can review, so nobody improves; managers who find out
about a stalled deal after the buyer has moved on.

## What YOUHAN ONE does about it

One workspace where the work is captured as it happens and moved along without a
person pushing it:

- **Leads arrive ready to work** — a spreadsheet becomes leads with its columns
  matched automatically; distribution rules hand them to the right seller.
- **Calls carry themselves** — inbound and outbound calls attach to the right lead,
  are recorded with consent, transcribed, summarised and scored; the coach suggests
  the next thing to say while the call is still going.
- **Follow-ups do not depend on memory** — every call and activity produces the
  next action; overdue work surfaces to the seller and the manager.
- **The day is accounted for** — check-in and check-out with face verification, and
  a check-out that waits until the day's assigned leads have been worked.
- **The owner sees it without asking** — assigned → contacted → calls → outcomes →
  deals per seller, attendance beside it, and what is still untouched.

## Measurable outcome

The front door and the platform console show what the platform has done, counted:
leads imported instead of typed, calls analysed by a model, automation steps that
ran, leads assigned by rule — and the hours those stand in for, using per-action
minutes that are stated in code and can be challenged. Per-seller productivity and
attendance give the owner the same view inside the workspace.

**Reduce manual work → automate repetitive tasks → guide sellers with AI → give
management visibility → close deals faster.** Before a feature is added, it is
asked: does it remove manual work, does it save time, can the platform do it, and
can the improvement be measured.
