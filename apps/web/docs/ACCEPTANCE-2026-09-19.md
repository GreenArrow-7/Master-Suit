## The complete journey, proven

`tests/e2e/business-journey.spec.ts` (PASS, 28 s with a warm worker): Company Admin
clones a role → creates a work location and an employee → records supervised consent →
a frame with no face is refused by the engine → imports a spreadsheet → assigns five
leads → the employee signs in and changes the temporary password → the check-out gate
reports 5 untouched → a call is placed through the vendor, consent recorded, transcript
uploaded, call completed → analysis and audit complete on the worker with a stated
model → the gate reports 4 → delete is refused, close-out succeeds → Productivity shows
1 call / 1 contacted for the employee → an opportunity is won → the Value view shows
deals won and leads imported.

## Not proven on this rig (external)

1. Face enrolment and face check-in with a real face — needs a photo/video of a
   consenting person for the engine; every other step is proven and the engine's refusal
   of a non-face is proven.
2. Model-quality of live hints/analysis/audit — needs `GEMINI_API_KEY`; run
   `npx tsx scripts/ai-eval.ts` and re-run the two e2e specs with the key present.
3. A real telephony vendor round-trip (Twilio/Exotel) — mock vendor proves the platform
   side; the vendor side is configuration in the customer's account.
